Research desk

Questions worth asking.

Use a repeatable framework to separate business performance from market sentiment and speculation.

01 / Fundamentals

What is driving performance?

Track revenue, gross and operating margins, cash flow, deliveries, energy storage and management guidance across comparable periods.

Use the earnings checklist ↗
02 / Valuation

What expectations are priced in?

Consider growth assumptions, profitability, future investment needs, competition and interest rates before drawing conclusions from a share price.

Read original disclosures ↗
03 / Risk framework

What could change the thesis?

Test both upside and downside scenarios: demand, pricing, execution, regulation, competition, technology and volatility.

Quarterly review

Look beyond the headline.

Build a habit of checking the reported figures, management commentary and original documents before relying on summaries.

Quarterly checklist

Review these indicators

  • Revenue and year-over-year comparisons
  • Gross margin and operating income
  • Operating cash flow, free cash flow and cash balance
  • Vehicle production and deliveries
  • Energy generation and storage deployments
  • Capital expenditure, outlook and risk disclosures
