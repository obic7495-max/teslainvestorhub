Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TeslaInvestorHub is an independent community for electric vehicles, technology and markets. It is not operated by or affiliated with Tesla, Inc. This policy explains what information the site processes when community features are enabled.

Information you provide

If you create an account, Supabase authentication processes your email address and sign-in credentials. The public profile stores display name, optional handle, optional avatar URL and optional bio. Posts, comments, likes, follows, stories, reports and chat messages are processed to provide the features you choose to use.

What is public and what is private

  • Posts, comments, public profile details, likes, follows, uploaded post images and active stories are available for visitors to view.
  • Direct messages are designed to be readable only by the sender and recipient.
  • Saved posts, notifications and reports are restricted to the relevant authenticated member under the database access policies.

Do not publish personal information, addresses, payment details, identity documents, passwords, one-time codes, private keys or information about another person without their permission.

Photos and uploads

Images attached to public posts and stories are stored in the app's Supabase Storage bucket and made publicly viewable so other visitors can see them. Upload only images you have permission to share. Stories are hidden after 24 hours; expired story records may remain in storage/database until cleaned up.

Service providers and browser storage

The app uses Supabase for authentication, database and file storage; TradingView for embedded market charts; and Wikimedia Commons for licensed vehicle photographs. These services process requests under their own terms and privacy notices. The site may use browser local storage to remember the light/dark theme and may cache the static app shell for installable-web-app support.

Security and retention

Database Row Level Security is intended to restrict member actions to permitted records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Content may remain until you delete it, it is removed under moderation rules, or the service owner deletes it. Copies shared or cached by other people may remain outside the site's control.

Your choices

You can choose what to post, update your public profile, remove your own posts where the interface offers that control, and stop using the service. For account deletion or privacy requests, contact the site operator using a contact method published on this site. The site operator should add a verified contact address before launch.

Children and sensitive information

The community is not designed to collect information from children. Do not use it to post highly sensitive personal or financial information.

Changes

This starter policy may be updated as features or legal obligations change. Replace this text with a reviewed policy that reflects the final service before promoting it to the public.
