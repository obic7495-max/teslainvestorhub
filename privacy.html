Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TeslaInvestorHub is an independent informational project. It is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc. This page describes the data practices intended for this static website. The owner should review and adapt it to the services actually enabled before public commercial launch.

Information you provide

If Supabase account registration and community chat are enabled, the authentication service may process your email address, account identifier, optional display name, and messages you choose to post. Do not share passwords, verification codes, payment details, or sensitive personal information in community posts.

Service providers and embedded content

This site may connect to Supabase for account functionality, TradingView for charts, Google News RSS through a scheduled workflow, Wikimedia Commons for licensed photographs, and YouTube for videos. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies and may receive technical data such as your IP address or browser information when your device connects to them.

Local settings and storage

The site may use browser local storage to remember your light/dark theme preference. It does not intentionally store passwords in local storage.

News and photos

News headlines are collected from public RSS feeds and link to publishers. Photos in the gallery are served from Wikimedia Commons and display links to each source and licence. Opening an external source takes you to that provider’s website.

Retention, security and contact

Account records and messages are retained by the connected Supabase project according to its configuration. No online system can guarantee perfect security; avoid posting private data. A specific privacy contact email has not yet been configured for this project. The owner should add a real monitored contact address before inviting the public.

Your choices

You may choose not to register, can avoid posting community messages, and can adjust browser storage settings. Account data requests should be directed to the website operator once a monitored contact address has been added.
